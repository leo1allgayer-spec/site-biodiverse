## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an avant-garde scientific and environmental engineering identity. Merging high-level environmental consultancy with rigorous industrial precision, the visual language balances deep ecological gravitas with clean, analytical structure. 

The aesthetic is grounded in absolute geometric discipline: stark zero-radius edges, surgical line-work, high-density data presentation, and technical typography. Rather than relying on generic soft naturalism, it conveys authority, environmental compliance, and high-spec biological engineering through structured technical layouts, monospaced metadata badges, and high-contrast structural hierarchy. The UI evokes confidence, statutory compliance, scientific exactitude, and corporate solidity.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high-density botanical depth paired with laboratory-grade neutrals:

- **Primary (`#134E39`)**: Deep scientific pine green. Used for authoritative institutional anchors, structural headers, high-emphasis interactive surfaces, and key brand marks.
- **Secondary (`#00A887`)**: Vibrant technical mint-emerald derived from environmental analytics. Deployed exclusively for functional data highlights, active states, progress indicators, and tactical primary CTAs.
- **Tertiary (`#182B36`)**: Deep industrial slate. Provides typographic weight, rigorous table lines, and grounding contrast for technical readouts.
- **Neutral (`#F4F6F4`)**: Sterile off-white neutral substrate that mitigates stark digital glare while maintaining pristine clinical legibility.
- **Dark Inversion (`#0B261C`)**: Deep nocturnal evergreen used for high-impact hero zones, statutory compliance banners, and footer frameworks.

## Typography

Typographic scale balances the architectural mechanics of **Space Grotesk** with the balanced neutrality of **Work Sans**. 

- **Space Grotesk** drives all headlines, metrics, operational telemetry, and navigation items. Its technical glyph contours reinforce engineering credibility. All micro-labels, technical tags, and process indicators are cast in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em`).
- **Work Sans** governs informational copy, environmental impact audits, long-form assessments, and data-entry fields, delivering high clarity at tight densities.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural grid with visible framing parameters.

- **System Grid**: A disciplined 12-column layout on desktop (max width `80rem` / 1280px) with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Mobile scales down to a single or 4-column module with `1rem` (16px) margins.
- **Cadence & Alignment**: Spatial intervals follow an absolute 8px modular scale (augmented by a 4px sub-step for technical metrics). Structural blocks use precise internal offsets (`space-xl`, `space-2xl`) combined with fine `1px` divisional grid borders rather than generous, formless padding.
- **Section Breaks**: Alternation between pure laboratory white (`#FFFFFF`), baseline off-white (`#F4F6F4`), and full-bleed institutional dark evergreen (`#0B261C`) defines categorical boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the technical aesthetic, depth is rendered through architectural surface layers and micro-line structuring rather than blurred dropshadows.

- **Surface Layering**: Elevation tiers are distinguished through subtle background shifts: Base canvas (`#F4F6F4`), Container Canvas (`#FFFFFF`), and Sub-recess (`#EBEFEA`).
- **Technical Outlines**: All interactive containers, data panels, and structural divisions use 1px crisp borders (`#D7DDD9`). Active or prioritized states advance forward by increasing line contrast to `#134E39` or `#00A887` rather than using volumetric diffusion.
- **Directional Offset Shadows**: Where tactile elevation is required for floating navigation headers or tactical dropdowns, the system employs hard, un-blurred geometric projection shadows: `0px 4px 0px 0px rgba(19, 78, 57, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

All corners across the design system maintain an unyielding `0px` radius. This applies universally across buttons, form inputs, technical badges, system cards, modal dialogues, and alert banners. This strict angular visual language delivers high-precision technical discipline, reflecting engineering blueprints, CAD schematics, and analytical rigour.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Sharp rect (`0px` radius), solid `#134E39` background, white label in Space Grotesk (`label-interactive`), `14px 28px` padding. Hover state: background transforms to `#00A887`, text retains `#FFFFFF`. Active state: inset border `2px solid #0B261C`.
- **Secondary Action**: Sharp rect, transparent fill, `1px solid #134E39`, `#134E39` label. Hover state: `#F4F6F4` fill with full black/pine contrast.
- **Technical Minimal**: Understated zero-border link accompanied by a terminal right arrow (`→`), weighted in Space Grotesk with `#00A887` accenting.

### Technical Badges & Chips
- **Structural Spec**: Absolute zero-radius tags, `4px 8px` padding. Upper-case Space Grotesk (`label-technical`).
- **Variant - Audit / Certified**: Background `#E6F5F1`, text `#00A887`, `1px solid #00A887`.
- **Variant - Neutral Data**: Background `#EAEFEA`, text `#182B36`, `1px solid #D7DDD9`.

### Cards & Grid Containers
- **Engineering Modular Card**: Sharp `0px` perimeter, pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`), framed by `1px solid #D7DDD9`. Internal padding at `24px`. 
- **Top Accent Modifier**: Premium cards (e.g., core compliance services) include an integrated `3px solid #134E39` top border. Hover state transitions the border to `#00A887` with an offset hard shadow (`0px 4px 0px #134E39`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Flat, sharp inputs, `44px` height, `1px solid #D7DDD9`, `#FFFFFF` surface. Active focus state removes default outline and engages a `2px solid #134E39` border.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Angular square footprints (`0px` radius for checkboxes, standard circle for radios). Unchecked: `1px solid #789D8B`. Checked: Solid `#134E39` with crisp `#FFFFFF` tick mark.

### Header & Navigation
- **Corporate Sticky Navigation**: Fixed top header (`72px` height) with `#FFFFFF` background, grounded by a subtle `1px solid #D7DDD9` bottom divider. Houses technical typography breadcrumbs, primary institutional navigation, and an immediate high-contrast quote CTA button.
